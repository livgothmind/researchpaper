Group managers (members of GestoreGruppi) can create research groups and assign members. Only admins (Django superusers) can promote / demote users, toggle group-manager rights, or delete accounts. Deleting a user wipes their group memberships, favorites and bot accounts, but preserves any papers they uploaded (attribution is cleared).
